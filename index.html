About Me

I'm Neeloy — part scientist, part debugger of life, full-time overthinker. I spend my days convincing neural networks to care about molecules and my nights convincing myself that Being and Time will start making sense on the next page. It never does. I keep reading anyway.

I run on four fuels: history, culture, science, and code. My work sits at the intersection of Machine Learning and Computational Biophysics — think protein folding, cheminformatics, and the kind of datasets that make your laptop fan sound like a jet engine. I also dabble in data science, because apparently one rabbit hole wasn't enough.

When I'm not staring at loss curves or yelling at Manchester United for conceding in the 89th minute, you'll find me quizzing (badly, but passionately), reading about civilisations that collapsed before they could invent version control, logging obscure films on Letterboxd, or adding books to my StoryGraph faster than I can read them. I keep a Reading Room — part resource dump, part philosophical crisis archive featuring Camus, Marcus Aurelius, and an ever-growing "To Read" pile that judges me silently.

The truth is out there. I'm just trying to fit a model to it.
